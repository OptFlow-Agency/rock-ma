# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 with Tailwind 4 and @lucide/astro (already scaffolded by the user).

## Users

Musicians, home-studio producers and gigging players in Morocco browsing for instruments, audio and studio gear, mostly on phones.

## Product Purpose

Rock Ma is an online music-gear store: guitars and basses, audio (microphones, headphones), studio equipment (controllers, interfaces). Success is a visitor entering a category or a product page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English copy, prices in DHS (Moroccan dirham), format "2.650 DHS".
- Product names, ratings, review counts and prices in the comp are placeholders until real catalog data exists.
- Mobile-first; the supplied comp covers hero to final CTA only. Desktop is derived from it.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Rock Ma" (wordmark set in Graen Metal).
- Fonts: Graen Metal (display) and Caudex (text), pinned by the user.
- Color palette pinned by the user: #313030, #BBBBBB-range silver, #E6E7E3, #030105, #202020, #8791A0, #1D1C17.
- Tagline copy from the comp: "Make your sound." / "Find your signature sound." / "Your studio, your rules."

## Evidence on Hand

Photography in `src/assets/` (hero, three category photos, CTA background) and four product cutouts (guitar, headphones, microphone, keyboard). No real reviews, catalog or pricing exist; do not invent more.

## Product Principles

1. Gear is the hero: photography and product cutouts lead, chrome recedes.
2. Browse by what you play: categories first, then proof via best sellers.
3. Clear path to buy: every card is a door to a category or product.
